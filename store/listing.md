
**Full description**

```
BillKul is a simple quotation and invoice maker for shops, traders, service providers and freelancers. Write a quote in about a minute, send it to your customer on WhatsApp, and turn it into an invoice with one tap.

MAKE A QUOTE IN A MINUTE
• Pick a customer, add your items, and the total is ready
• Your items and prices are remembered, so the next quote is faster
• Add a discount, tax and a note for the customer
• Everything is saved as you type

SEND IT YOUR WAY
• Send as a PDF or as a picture on WhatsApp
• Print it or save it as a PDF
• Two clean templates with your shop name, phone number and logo

FROM QUOTE TO INVOICE
• Convert an accepted quote into an invoice in one tap, with nothing to type again
• Mark an invoice as paid when the money arrives
• See what you quoted, invoiced and received this month, and what is still due

CUSTOMERS AND PRICE LIST
• Keep your customers in one place and see who still owes you
• Add a customer from your phone contacts
• Change a price in your price list in two taps

MADE FOR THE WAY YOU WORK
• Works without internet
• English and Urdu, with full right-to-left layout
• Your own currency and document numbers
• Save a backup file and restore it on a new phone

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
BillKul needs no sign-up. Your customers, prices and documents are kept on your own phone.

Documents made with BillKul are quotations and bills. They are not tax invoices for sales tax purposes.
```
