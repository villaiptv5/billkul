Privacy policy

Last updated %DATE%

The short version

BillKul is a quotation and invoice maker. Everything you type into it stays on your own phone or computer. BillKul has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and we never receive your shop details, your customers or your documents.

What BillKul stores, and where

BillKul stores what you enter so that the app can work:

  • your shop name, phone number, address and logo;
  • your customers' names, phone numbers and addresses;
  • your items, prices and stock counts;
  • your quotations and invoices;
  • your cash book and expenses, and your settings.

In the Android app this is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. In the web version it is kept in your browser's storage on that computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you ask for one in Settings, and you choose where to keep it. Anyone who has that file can read your data, so keep it somewhere safe.

If backup is turned on in your phone's Android settings, Android may include BillKul's data in your phone's own backup, in your own Google account. That backup is run by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Contacts. Asked for only when you choose to add a customer from your phone's contacts. BillKul copies the name and phone number of the one contact you pick. Your contact list is not uploaded or copied.
  • Photos. When you add a shop logo, you pick one picture. BillKul keeps a small copy of it on your device for your documents.

BillKul does not use your camera, microphone or location.

When you send a document

When you send a quotation or invoice on WhatsApp, share it with another app, or print it, the document is handed to the app or printer you chose. From there it is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

What goes over the internet

BillKul works without an internet connection. When there is a connection:

  • the document preview downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives the request, including your IP address, as any website does. Nothing about your shop, customers or documents is sent;
  • the web version is loaded from our web hosting, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, time and the page requested) for security and troubleshooting.

BillKul contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

Deleting your data

You can delete any document, customer or item inside BillKul. Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything BillKul stored on that device. Backup files you saved stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

Children

BillKul is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Features planned for later, such as signing in with Google and automatic backup to your own Google Drive, will change what BillKul does with data. This page will be updated, with a new date at the top, before any such feature is released.
