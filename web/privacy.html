Privacy policy

The short version

BillKul is a quotation and invoice maker. What you type into it (your shop details, customers, items, documents and cash book) stays on your own phone or computer, and we never receive it. To use BillKul you sign in with your mobile number; our server keeps that number, your plan, and a count of how much of the free allowance you have used. BillKul has no advertising and no tracking.

What BillKul stores, and where

BillKul stores what you enter so that the app can work:

  • your shop name, phone number, address and logo;
  • your customers' names, phone numbers and addresses;
  • your items, prices and stock counts;
  • your quotations and invoices;
  • your cash book and expenses, and your settings.

In the Android app this is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. In the web version it is kept in your browser's storage on that computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Your BillKul account

The first time, you sign in with your mobile number and a code sent to that number on WhatsApp, and you choose a password. After that you sign in with your number and password; if you forget it, a new code lets you choose another. Our own server, on our own web hosting, keeps:

  • your mobile number;
  • your plan (Free or Pro) and, for Pro, the date it runs to;
  • how many quotations and invoices, and how many cash book entries, you have made. These are counts only: not the documents, their amounts or your customers;
  • a one-way fingerprint of the ID Android gives BillKul on your phone (in the web version, a random ID kept in your browser), with the same counts for that phone. It lets a new number signed in on the same phone carry on from what the phone already used. The ID itself is not kept and cannot be worked back from the fingerprint;
  • the version of BillKul you use, whether it is the Android app or the web version, and when you last opened it;
  • your password, scrambled one way (hashed) so that no one, us included, can read it;
  • a sign-in code for up to 10 minutes while you are signing in.

We use this to sign you in, to apply the free allowance and to switch Pro on for your number. We do not sell it or share it with anyone. If you contact us about Pro on WhatsApp, WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own privacy policy.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you ask for one in Settings, and you choose where to keep it. Anyone who has that file can read your data, so keep it somewhere safe.

If backup is turned on in your phone's Android settings, Android may include BillKul's data in your phone's own backup, in your own Google account. That backup is run by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Backup to your own Google Drive

In the Android app you can choose a Gmail account for an automatic backup. BillKul then saves one backup file, "BillKul backup.json" in a "BillKul" folder, to that account's Google Drive, and writes over it when your data changes. The file goes straight from your phone to your Google Drive: it does not pass through our server, and we cannot see it.

BillKul asks Google only for permission to see and change the files BillKul itself makes on your Drive. It cannot see your other files. The Gmail address is kept only on your phone, so the app can show where your backup goes. You can stop the Drive backup in Settings at any time; the file already on your Drive stays until you delete it. Google stores the file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Contacts. Asked for only when you choose to add a customer from your phone's contacts. BillKul copies the name and phone number of the one contact you pick. Your contact list is not uploaded or copied.
  • Photos. When you add a shop logo, you pick one picture. BillKul keeps a small copy of it on your device for your documents.

BillKul does not use your camera, microphone or location.

When you send a document

When you send a quotation or invoice on WhatsApp, share it with another app, or print it, the document is handed to the app or printer you chose. From there it is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

What goes over the internet

BillKul works without an internet connection once you have signed in. When there is a connection:

  • the document preview downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives the request, including your IP address, as any website does. Nothing about your shop, customers or documents is sent;
  • BillKul tells our server the counts described under "Your BillKul account" and learns whether your number is on Free or Pro;
  • the web version is loaded from our web hosting, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, time and the page requested) for security and troubleshooting.

BillKul contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

Deleting your data and your account

You can delete any document, customer or item inside BillKul. Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything BillKul stored on that device. Backup files you saved stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

To delete your account: open BillKul, go to Settings, and choose "Delete my account". Your mobile number is removed from our server at once. If you cannot open the app, write to us at the contact below from the same number and we will delete it for you.

After an account is deleted we keep two things, neither of which can be read back into a number or a phone: a one-way fingerprint of the number, and the one-way fingerprint of the phone, each with the count of free allowance used, so that deleting and signing up again does not start a new free allowance.

Children

BillKul is a business tool and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If BillKul starts doing anything new with data, this page will be updated, with a new date at the top, before that feature is released.
